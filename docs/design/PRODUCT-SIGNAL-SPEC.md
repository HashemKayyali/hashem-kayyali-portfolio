# Product Signal — ambitious homepage redirection

User explicitly rejects the previous safe masthead and requests a fresh creative leap, strong GSAP, immediate portrait, responsive composition and no oversized laptop typography/margins. Execute autonomously. Checkpoint before changes: output/checkpoints/design-intelligence-before-2026-10-13-171934.zip, 678 verified files.

## Critique and chosen direction
Previous Home gives almost all first-impression space to a name and a product photograph. Its portrait is a tiny late-loading footnote and disappears on mobile. Most motion consists of simple opacity/translation; sections feel like an editorial template rather than a designed interaction sequence.

Product Signal brings the engineer forward: compact asymmetric cream hero, large immediate real portrait with cobalt backplate and real product fragments, restrained charcoal display typography, green physical-system chapter, lavender software chapter and warm operational chapter. Coral contact provides a distinct closing scene. No invented statistics, simulated dashboards or generated portrait.

## Composition and interaction contract
- Home only; approved/creative Home retain identical design and existing routes/locales. Scoped CSS and Home-owned GSAP.
- Hero: portrait eager/high-priority, immediately visible, never opacity0/masked by entrance. Title max86px on laptop; canvas gutters24px. Photo is visible within the first mobile viewport too, with concise roles/actions adjacent/above.
- Entrance: word masks, expanding cobalt plate behind portrait, fanning project-photo fragments, detail/caption staggers. Portrait remains stationary/visible until compositor depth motion is armed after intro.
- Scroll: native scrolling; desktop sticky project scenes overlap with controlled predecessor scaling, image depth, label reveals and progress rule. Mobile retains ordinary flow and modest image depth; no pin/scroll hijack. SVG connector is decorative scope art, not an invented engineering diagram.
- Focus/pointer: visible native project anchors; focus brings obscured sticky-card content into view. Controlled button feedback and hover image lens; touch uses the same native destinations. All IDs/status semantics are real project scope.
- Reduced motion/live toggle: static visible content, no sticky pile-up, no active tweens/triggers; cleanups revert timelines, delayed callbacks and listeners. Low-power/save-data policy stays honored.
- Preserve all genuine dictionaries/data/media. ProductImage and StoryMedia protect sensitive NFC/bike interfaces.
