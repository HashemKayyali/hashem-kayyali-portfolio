What changed

The shared fluid renderer now has a smoke treatment based on domain-warped noise. Dense folds use the same #58152D Burgundy and #3E1022 Wine as the identity; lighter vapor uses neutral #AAA09B. Edge feathering happens inside the shader density instead of translucent Wine overlays that washed toward rose.

Smoke appears behind the Hero portrait, below the About reading area as a transition into Projects, behind the Eventies composition and at the contact invitation. One shared WebGL context paints the visible smoke surfaces, capped at12fps desktop /8fps mobile. Mobile noise uses two octaves; reduced motion and constrained devices receive a quiet, static Canvas fallback.
