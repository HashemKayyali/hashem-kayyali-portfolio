Everything below runs on the local build (not deployed). Before = live hashemkayyali.com, after = local. Logo directions: HK logo exploration.

01Hero smoke: no jump, no flashing line

Cause. The smoke canvas was resized to every hero's shape, so its composition was recalculated at the end of each page change (the “dumb jump”), and its hard edge showed as a line. While a lazy page loaded, the old hero also reported 0px and the smoke dipped.

Fix. The smoke is now a fixed field sized to the viewport; the hero is a window over it that glides to the next height. The vertical fades live on the window, so its edges are always soft. Lazy pages and Studio pages are followed correctly; zero-height readings are ignored.

02Back: no scroll jump on the current page

Cause. GSAP ScrollTrigger remembers the browser’s scroll-restoration mode from load time (“auto”) and writes it back on every refresh. On Back, the browser therefore scrolled the current page to the previous page’s position before the app switched pages.

Fix. ScrollTrigger is told the mode is manual; the app alone restores positions.

03“From the requirement to a working product”

Each step now owns a scroll beat, so the pinned board stays on screen and changes image three times. The step switches as its text reaches the board. The panel is bounded (400–640px) with a larger image and clearer type scale.

04Selected work cards share one height

All Home cards use the same media box (16:10, gentle centre crop), so Bike Tower and Basket Beats now align and the next section no longer shows early.

Cards enter from their own side of the row (left from the left, right from the right), centred cards rise with depth; the photograph unmasks from below and settles from a slight zoom. Same-row cards stagger by 90ms.
